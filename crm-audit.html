Data Audit & Transformation

I led a SQL-driven CRM data rescue initiative that transformed fragmented, unreliable records into a structured, ready intelligence system supporting sales growth and marketing performance.

This project didn’t just clean data, it unlocked the full potential of the CRM.

The Problem

CRM reporting relied on data that was incomplete, inconsistent, and difficult to trust for decision-making.


The CRM had significant gaps that affected reporting and decision-making:

  • Many fields were mostly empty: 50 fields >98% missing, 16 partially missing, only 16 fully complete
  • Industry missing for >31% of the total records, there are over 600 distinct industries
  • Job Title missing for >35% of the total records, there are more than 2000 distinct job titles
  • Lead Status missing for >50% of the records
  • Email/Lead Source missing for more than 2,000 records

Without reliable data, it was difficult to measure lead conversion, segment accounts, track revenue, or identify at-risk clients. Large gaps in key information, such as Industry, Company Size, and activity frequency, prevented the business from learning from the data and using it to guide strategy.

The Solution

A SQL-driven data quality and enrichment workflow was implemented to clean, standardise, and enhance CRM data.


Check data completeness:

  • Counted missing values, percentages, and distinct entries for key fields
  • Evaluated patterns of missing or inconsistent data to prioritize quality improvements
  • Assessed potential impact of data gaps on reporting accuracy and decision-making

Fill missing data:

  • Used email domains with external tables (Gemini) to estimate missing Company Size and Industry
  • Standardised over 600 unique industries into 26 clear categories
  • Cleaned and standardised Job Title values
  • Used business logic to apply the correct lead status
  • Used a detailed lead source field to fill in missing lead source values

Prepare client and account analysis:

  • Created views to categorize accounts as New, Existing, Returning, Lost, or Prospect
  • Calculated total revenue won and assigned Tier 1–4 classifications
  • Tracked opportunity frequency and flagged accounts with no recent activity for potential churn

Monitor lead conversion:

  • Combined converted and unconverted leads, calculated lead age, and tracked if opportunities happened before conversion to evaluate marketing and sales performance

Sample SQL Diagnostics

SQL queries were used to audit CRM data completeness and industry mapping coverage before enrichment.

  • Data Quality Summary: Counted missing values and distinct entries for key lead fields.
  • Industry Gap Analysis: Classified industries as Missing, Needs Mapping, or Correct to measure standardization coverage.

Results & Impact

The cleaned and enriched CRM data enabled more reliable reporting and actionable insight across sales and marketing.

  • Filling critical gaps and standardising data significantly improved completeness and accuracy.
  • Industry and lead source cleanup made segmentation and reporting more reliable.
  • Client classification, revenue tiers, and churn flags provided actionable insights to prioritise high-value and at-risk accounts.
  • Opportunity frequency and lead conversion tracking enabled proactive engagement and better marketing/sales decisions.

Overall, the project transformed unreliable CRM data into a trusted analytical foundation, enabling 100% data-driven decisions and directly supporting improved lead conversion, account management, and revenue growth.
