Commercial Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of commercial performance in 2024 across 15+ European countries. It tracks key financial and operational KPIs, visualizes gross profit trends, analyzes trade lane and product performance, and monitors customer tier and retention patterns, enabling stakeholders to make informed commercial decisions.


Project Overview

Objective

Develop an executive-level dashboard to monitor commercial performance, analyze profitability by product, trade lane, and customer tier, and support data-driven decision-making across the European region, while providing tailored, country-specific views for local teams.

Toolstack

Power BI for interactive visualization, DAX and Power Query for data modeling and transformation, with data sourced from ERP, CRM, and finance systems. Analytical techniques include waterfall analysis, cohort analysis, decomposition trees, and trade lane-level comparisons.

Audience

Commercial leadership, Sales and Account Management teams, Regional Managers, and Strategy and Business Development teams. The dashboard provides both high-level KPIs for executive oversight and detailed views for operational and strategic decisions, with access tailored via row-level security.

Dashboard Preview

The dashboard features interactive KPI cards summarizing revenue, gross profit, total trade lanes, tonnage, and revenue per ton. Line and waterfall visuals track gross profit trends and product-level performance, while bar charts and decomposition trees provide deep trade lane insights. Donut charts and structured tables analyze customer tier distribution, retention patterns, and account upgrades, enabling rapid identification of performance drivers and commercial risks.


Row-level security (RLS) ensures controlled access across 15+ countries. Managing Directors automatically view their respective country performance with interactive drill-down capabilities, while executive leadership maintains consolidated regional visibility.


Retention definition: Retention is measured as the percentage of the original cohort active each month; as this is a transactional logistics business, clients may skip months and return later, which can lead to increases in retention in subsequent periods.

Process

Data Sources & Preparation

  • Consolidated commercial and financial data from ERP, CRM, and internal reporting systems.
  • Prepared analytical tables for KPIs, trade lanes, tonnage, and customer tiers.
  • Segmented clients by tier and cohort for retention and profitability analysis.
  • Standardized trade lane, product, and regional data for consistent reporting across 15+ European countries.

Dashboard Development & Design

  • Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard with slicers to filter by country and explore each market.
  • Built KPI cards, line charts, waterfall charts, bar charts, donut charts, tables, and decomposition trees.
  • Used advanced DAX formulas to support dynamic filtering and interactive analysis.
  • Enabled drill-downs by product, trade lane, and tier for actionable commercial insights.

Key Insights

  • Gross profit trends and measures highlight performance across region.
  • Waterfall and trade lane analyses identify high-impact products and flows.
  • Customer tier and retention analysis reveal opportunities for upselling and loyalty improvements.
  • Top upgraders and decomposition trees support targeted account and product strategies.

Impact

  • Supports strategic and operational decision-making across the European region.
  • Reduces manual reporting and consolidates multi-country performance in one dashboard.
  • Enables proactive revenue management, client retention, and trade lane optimization.
  • Provides a single source of truth for KPIs, gross profit, trade lanes, tonnage, and tier insights.

Note: All metrics presented are based on professional experience and have been anonymised, aggregated, or proportionally adjusted to ensure compliance with confidentiality and data protection standards.
