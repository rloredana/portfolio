Revenue & Client Insights

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of revenue performance and client behaviour for 2025. It tracks key KPIs, monitors client acquisition, retention, and churn, and analyses revenue trends by industry, lead source, client status, and tier, enabling stakeholders to identify growth opportunities and manage client relationships effectively.


Project Overview

Objective

Develop an executive dashboard to monitor revenue and client KPIs, track trends, and analyse client acquisition, retention, churn, and revenue tiers. Provides actionable insights into client status, lead sources, and industry performance to support commercial decision-making.

Toolstack

Power BI for interactive visualisation; SQL, DAX and Power Query for data modelling and transformation. Analytical techniques include trend analysis, revenue contribution assessment by client status and tier, and performance comparisons by industry and lead source.

Audience

Commercial leadership, Sales and Account Management teams, and Strategy teams. The dashboard provides high-level KPIs for executive oversight and detailed insights for operational and strategic decisions.

Dashboard Preview

The dashboard presents interactive KPI cards summarizing Revenue, New Clients, Average Deal Size, Win Rate, and Churn Rate, providing an immediate snapshot of commercial performance. Line charts compare 2024 vs. 2025 revenue trends to highlight growth momentum and seasonal patterns.


Bar charts and structured tables analyze revenue contribution and year-over-year growth by industry and lead source, identifying high-performing acquisition channels. Stacked and segmented bar visuals illustrate client distribution, revenue contribution, and impact on YoY growth by client status and tier, enabling deeper insight into portfolio quality. Donut visuals surface clients at risk of churn, supporting proactive retention strategies and revenue protection.

Process

Data Sources & Preparation

  • Extracted and consolidated data from CRM and financial systems into MySQL database.
  • Structured and normalized data into analytical tables for reporting.
  • Integrated client, deal, and revenue data using joins and CTEs.
  • Classified clients into New, Returning, and Existing groups for retention analysis.
  • Segmented clients by purchase frequency and revenue tiers to identify behaviour patterns and categorise contribution.
  • Applied churn flag and logic to identify at-risk clients.
  • Standardised industry and lead source to ensure consistency.
  • Created SQL views to streamline data delivery to Power BI dashboards.

Dashboard Development & Design

  • Developed a Power BI dashboard with real-time data connections to MySQL database.
  • Designed KPI cards for Revenue, Average Deal Size, New Clients, Win Rate, and Churn Rate.
  • Built visualisations including line charts for trends, bar charts for industry performance, tables for lead source efficiency, and stacked bar charts for client distribution.
  • Implemented DAX calculations for dynamic metrics: YoY growth, revenue contribution, client classification insights, and purchase frequency analysis.
  • Ensured dashboard interactivity for exploring KPIs by industry, client status, lead source, and revenue tier.

Key Insights

  • High-Value Clients: Existing clients generating frequent purchases account for a significant portion of total revenue.
  • Revenue Growth: Total revenue increased substantially YoY, despite a decline in new client acquisition and win rate.
  • Outbound Sales Performance: Outbound channels contributed the majority of revenue growth.
  • Industry Performance: Consumer and Retail sectors are leading growth, while Public Sector, Financial Services, and HR show declining revenue.

Impact

  • Provides visibility on high-value clients and potential retention opportunities.
  • Supports strategic decision-making by highlighting growth drivers by industry, lead source, and client segments.
  • Reduces time spent on manual reporting and data consolidation.
  • Enables proactive revenue management, client acquisition, and retention planning.
  • Empowers teams to optimise sales and marketing strategies based on client behaviour and channel performance.

Note: All metrics presented are based on professional experience and have been anonymised, aggregated, or proportionally adjusted to ensure compliance with confidentiality and data protection standards.
